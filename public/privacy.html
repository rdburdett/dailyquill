Daily Quill privacy policy

Daily Quill shows a quote on each new tab. Your settings are stored in your browser through Chrome's sync storage and are never sent to us.

Anonymous usage stats

Unless you turn it off in Settings ("Share anonymous usage stats"), Daily Quill sends Google Analytics a small event when you open a new tab, ask for another quote, or change your quote sources. Each event includes a random ID created for your browser install, the name of the quote source shown, and the extension version.

We do not send quote text, the pages you visit, your browsing history, your name, email or any account details. Google may process your IP address to estimate a rough location, as described in Google's privacy policy. The data is used only to understand how Daily Quill is used and to improve it. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Quote sources

Some quotes are fetched from ZenQuotes and other public quote services. Those requests contain no personal information.
